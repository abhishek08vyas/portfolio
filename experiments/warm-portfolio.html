Approach

From incident pager to idea, three habits.

01

Understand first

Every system I've fixed started with listening — to the logs, the metrics, and the people using it. SRE years taught me that.

02

Build boring, ship calm

Blue-Green deploys, tested paths, honest error handling. The best releases are the ones nobody notices.

03

Observe everything

If it isn't measured, it isn't done. Dashboards and alerts ship with the feature, not after the outage.
